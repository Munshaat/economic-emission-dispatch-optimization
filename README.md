# Economic and Emission Dispatch Optimization of a Six-Generator Power System

**Type:** Group academic engineering project  
**Course:** EEE 4631 — Power System III, Islamic University of Technology (IUT)  
**Tools:** MATLAB (reported methodology)  
**Topics:** Economic Load Dispatch (ELD), Economic Emission Dispatch (EED), Combined Economic and Emission Dispatch (CEED)

## Project Description
This project examines how electricity generation can be distributed among six thermal power generators to meet a 900 MW demand while reducing fuel cost and emissions. MATLAB and the lambda-iteration method were used to compare economic load dispatch, emission dispatch, and a combined approach. The report presents the resulting generator allocations and compares operating costs with emissions.

## Reported results
| Strategy | Total cost ($/h) | Total emissions (kg/h) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Economic Load Dispatch (ELD) | 9,579.73 | 16,719.24 |
| Combined dispatch (CEED, w = 0.5) | 9,605.00 | 14,162.10 |
| Economic Emission Dispatch (EED) | 9,687.10 | 13,044.50 |

These are values **reported in the assignment**, not independently rerun or verified here. The report includes a Pareto trade-off figure, generator dispatch comparisons, and other plots.
